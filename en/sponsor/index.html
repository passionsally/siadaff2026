Partner Benefits

What sponsors gain from SIADAFF

Brand visibility

Logo and partner naming can appear on the official website, proposal materials, festival communications, event signage, and press assets.

Creative campaign briefs

Your product, service, policy, or campaign theme can become a creator challenge that generates diverse short-form ideas.

Global creator access

International entrants will be guided through an English FilmFreeway listing, while Korean entrants purchase an entry pass and submit through the official SIADAFF website.

ESG and creator support

Support individual youth entrants (ages 10–19), individual adult entrants (age 20+), and company teams while linking your brand to culture, education, and industry.

Awarded works usage

Awarded and submitted works may be screened, posted, and edited by the organizer for non-commercial promotional purposes within agreed boundaries.

Ceremony and PR exposure

Partner introductions, award naming, ceremony visibility, press references, and SNS content can create repeated touchpoints.

Schedule

2026 festival timeline

Jun 1-Jul 30

Sponsor Selection and Brand Theme Finalization

After sponsorship is confirmed and payment is completed, partners submit preferred campaign concepts and themes to the organizer. Final brand briefs are published on the official landing page.

Sep 28

Submission opens

Creator submissions begin with partner-linked themes and festival communication.

Oct 20

Submission closes

The official submission period closes at 23:59 KST.

Oct 24-Nov 12

Preliminary and final judging

Entries are reviewed for eligibility, theme fit, creativity, message delivery, and production quality.

Nov 21

Awards ceremony

The ceremony is planned in Gangnam, Seoul with partner and award winner exposure.

2026 · 1st Edition

Impact sharing and donation

Share the outcomes of the first edition and connect festival achievements to a social-impact donation initiative.

After the Ceremony

Winning works and partner promotion

Extend the reach of awarded works and partner achievements through official channels and coordinated promotional content.

2027 · 2nd Edition

Planning seminar

Hold a planning seminar to shape the second SIADAFF edition and its partner collaboration programs.
