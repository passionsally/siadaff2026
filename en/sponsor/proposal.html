SIADAFF is a Korean creative festival connecting AI, advertising, film, and short-form creators with brand and public-sector campaigns. It gives partners a practical way to turn a message into creator participation, shareable content, and festival-stage visibility.

Why Partner

A Korean creative festival with global creator access

Brand partnership, not just logo exposure

Partner messages can become creative briefs for posters, advertising films, and short-form dramas. This creates participatory campaign value beyond passive sponsorship.

Global submission channel

International creators will be guided through an English FilmFreeway listing, while Korean entrants purchase an entry pass and submit through the official SIADAFF website.

Creative talent discovery

SIADAFF welcomes individual youth entrants (ages 10–19), individual adult entrants (age 20+), and company teams, creating a broad talent pool for brand and public messages.

Festival and media moments

Partners can be connected to website exposure, press materials, SNS content, award naming, ceremony visibility, and post-event promotional use.

Six Sponsorship Types

Select and combine multiple sponsorship options

Partners may combine cash, products, services, infrastructure, and on-site programs to create a tailored partnership.

Type 01 · Cash

Cash Sponsorship

Official sponsor level, promotional exposure, brand briefs, and ceremony benefits are tailored to the sponsorship amount.

Type 02 · In-kind

In-kind Sponsorship

Provide products, equipment, production materials, packages, or services for the ceremony, audience, and entrants.

Type 03 · Merchandise

Official Apparel Sponsorship

Support staff and guest T-shirts, official apparel, bags, caps, and other visible branded merchandise.

Type 04 · Online Hall

Online Brand Hall

Use a dedicated official-site brand page for ongoing product, video, campaign, and event exposure.

Type 05 · On-site Seminar

On-site Seminar Sponsorship

Plan creator seminars or masterclasses and operate the session under the sponsor brand name.

Type 06 · Brand Party

Brand Party

Host a sponsor-themed networking party and on-site program for audiences and invited guests.
